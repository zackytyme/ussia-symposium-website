About the Symposium

The symposium brings together entrepreneurs, manufacturers, business leaders, policymakers and other stakeholders to discuss opportunities and challenges facing small-scale industries in Uganda.

Through presentations, panel discussions and networking, participants can explore practical ways to strengthen businesses, improve productivity and create employment.

Why Attend?

  • Learn from industry experts and experienced entrepreneurs.
  • Discover opportunities for business growth and expansion.
  • Network with manufacturers, suppliers and business partners.
  • Explore innovation and digital transformation.
  • Discuss trade and sustainable industrialisation.
  • Build connections that support business development.
